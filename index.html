Our Story

A Taste of Royal Awadhi Heritage

Nestled in the historic heart of Madiyon, Lucknow, Golden Blossom brings to life the timeless culinary traditions of the Nawabi era. Every recipe is a love letter to the city's 300-year-old food culture.

Our chefs trained under the finest ustads of Lucknow use age-old dum techniques, hand-ground masalas, and locally sourced fresh produce to craft dishes that transcend the ordinary.
